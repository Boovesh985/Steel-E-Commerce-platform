## Brand & Style

The brand personality is **engineered, resilient, and precise**. Designed for the industrial sector, it balances a "heavy-duty" aesthetic with the efficiency of a high-performance e-commerce platform. The visual style merges **Corporate/Modern** precision with subtle **Brutalist** structural elements — hairline borders, rigid alignment.
